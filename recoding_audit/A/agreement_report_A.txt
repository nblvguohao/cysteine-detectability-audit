    Coder A had no access to the papers. Every row was coded from the
    title, modification, study type and the recorded evidence quotations
    only. Measured over the 74 records: 51 quotations are cut off mid-clause
    (no terminal punctuation) and 0 are empty. In most of the truncated
    cases the missing clause is the one that would have named the background, so
    a background statement present in the paper may be absent from the record.
    For 1 of the 74 rows (uid 828) the recorded background evidence is a reagent
    sentence and is off-topic, which is the single U code.
    Coder A therefore cannot detect a background statement that exists in a
    paper but was not captured by the original regex extraction, so coder A's
    counts are lower bounds, not point estimates.
