# Supplemental Figures S1–S7: legends

Figures S1, S2, S3 and S6 show the four-protease deposit, the PXD015307 re-search, the self-audit and the planted-artifact benchmark in full (files `FigS1_public_four_protease.pdf`, `FigS2_search_space.pdf`, `FigS3_self_audit_public.pdf` and `FigS6_synthetic_ground_truth.pdf`). Figures S4, S5 and S7 show the protein-level claim re-tests, background choice and attribute definition, and the survey flow. Reference numbers refer to the main reference list.

**Figure S1. The same audit on a public deposit in which one enrichment was digested with four proteases (PXD063463).** **a**, cleavage-geometry log2 odds ratio against the proteome background, one row per arm in each band: a filled circle is that arm's own cleavage rule, an open circle the trypsin rule, and the grey segment between them is what the trypsin rule loses. The signature is present in the distal band (6 to 12 residues) and lost when a non-tryptic arm is scored with the trypsin rule; in the proximal band the two rules nearly coincide only for chymotrypsin (0.50 and 0.40) and differ for AspN (1.11 and 0.11) and GluC (0.52 and −0.01), and the trypsin arm, having no rule to differ from, carries one circle. Intervals are protein-clustered bootstraps at the Bonferroni level for the two bands (97.5% rather than 95%). **b**, the same statistic in the distal band against the observed background, the identified cysteines that carry no site assignment: no interval lies above zero (the proximal band is reported in Supplemental Note 4). **c**, the share of identified cysteines carrying a site assignment with its 95% interval; the dashed lines are the (non-blind) decision levels 0.90 and 0.50 for the Artifact 4 diagnostic, fixed after the primary deposit's two candidate statistics had been inspected (Supplemental Note 7). Arm sizes differ by an order of magnitude (Supplemental Note 4). The distal band of **a**, panel **b** and panel **c** reappear in main Fig. 3c, Fig. 3d and Fig. 4a. Source data are provided.

**Figure S2. What a search can decide between sulfide and cysteine dioxidation (PXD015307).** **a**, the 0.017758-Da separation in ppm of peptide neutral mass (curve) against precursor windows of ±10, ±4.5 and ±2 ppm (dashed lines); for an unbiased measurement the window admits both candidates to the right of each crossing (shaded), and each crossing gives, in parentheses, the share of theoretical tryptic cysteine peptides of the mouse proteome (7–30 residues, up to two missed cleavages, one +32 modification) at or below that mass. The bars below give the mass up to which each re-searched file's own precursor precision (the SD of its 1%-FDR PSMs) separates the two candidates by 2 SD on either side. **b**, precursor errors of the deposit's own 53 PSMs in the insulin files (none in the control file), colored by insulin chain, with the four sulfide-assigned PSMs as diamonds. Below, each sulfide-assigned PSM against the error expected under sulfide (circle, the baseline median) and under dioxidation (square, the baseline plus the separation: 7.0 ppm for the A chain, 5.0 ppm for the B chain), filled where the PSM lies within 2 SD of that expectation, with the reading at the right. A-chain PSMs are compared with the B-chain PSMs of their file (n = 12) and with the deposit's two A-chain PSMs without sulfide, using the file SD; with the SD of those two PSMs, the +4.60-ppm PSM reads neither. B-chain PSMs are compared with the deposit's 47 B-chain PSMs and read the same under all eight baselines examined (Supplemental Note 6). **c**, PSMs at 1% FDR per file in the re-search with both candidates searched; the +32-class count is zero in all five files. **d**, share of single-cysteine spectra with both candidates inside the ±10-ppm window whose two interpretations tie at the best score, by best E-value, for 1.0005-Da (ion-trap MS2, insulin files) and 0.02-Da (FTMS MS2, Fig-1D) fragment bins; numbers under the axis are tied over scored spectra. Panels **a**, **b** and **d** are post hoc. Values are drawn from the stored analysis tables, and the only computed element is the separation curve in **a**. Panels **a** (curve and windows) and **d** reappear in main Fig. 4b, c. Source data are provided.

**Figure S3. Self-audit of the ranker on the two public cohorts.** Color marks the cohort each value is measured on, rice PXD072089 (orange) or human PXD044043 (blue): in **a** and **d** it is audited zero-shot by the ranker trained on the other, in **b** and **c** each arm is refit within it. **a**, within-label-stratum AUC against the distal (6–12-residue) trypsin cleavage band for the chemistry set (colored) and the full feature set (gray). **b**, top-100 distal log2 odds ratios with asymmetric 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals for the ablation arms and a label-permuted control (gray), read through the LightGBM ranking member refit within each cohort (Supplemental Note 11); the reference level for this statistic is the value that label inheritance alone produces, about 1.2 in human and 0.76 in rice, not zero (Supplemental Note 21; Table S1). **c**, within-protein AUC of that member for the chemistry set, the two ablation arms, the detectability-only set and the full set, one point per cohort; the dashed line is 0.5. The detectability-only and full-set values reappear in main Fig. 6b. **d**, within-label-positive-stratum AUC across five proteases for the chemistry set, both directions. Intervals in **a**, **b** and **d** are 95% cluster-bootstraps from 5,000 replicates, clustered by protein. Structural features are absent from the external sequences, so comparisons in **b** and **c** are within cohort and across arms. Source data are provided.

**Figure S4. Re-tests of the ten protein-level claims.** These claims are not estimable without a protein-level negative class, which none of their cohorts provides (Table 1, item 6), and they are not counted with the site-level verdicts of main Fig. 5; their re-test estimates are shown for completeness. Nine claims are drawn as log2 odds ratios; the inset gives `PERS-010` on its own estimand (observed over expected), not comparable with the odds ratios. An open circle marks the reconstructed baseline and a filled circle the matched estimate, with the matched estimate's 95% cluster-bootstrap interval; the vertical line is no effect. Gray type gives the stored re-test verdict (Supplemental Data 2). `PERS-008` is drawn against its authors' zinc-finger background; its whole-proteome reading is in Fig. S5a. Source data are provided.

**Figure S5. Background choice and attribute definition in the claim re-tests.** **a**, the same claim (`PERS-008`) measured against the authors' own zinc-finger-eligible background and against the whole-proteome background, with a matched and a same-size random control. **b**, the measured composition of the positive and negative sets for five structural claims; "DSSP vs P-SEA" marks a claim whose secondary-structure categories were assigned in the original study with DSSP [87], which could not be reproduced here, where structure is assigned with P-SEA [34]. Colors of the matched estimates in **a** and of the claims in **b** give their verdicts, as in main Fig. 5. Points and intervals are drawn from the analysis tables; intervals are 95% percentile intervals from 5,000 cluster-bootstrap replicates. The two panels use different estimands and are not placed on a common axis. Source data are provided.

**Figure S6. The audit answers a planted artifact in proportion to its size.** **a**, detection rate against injected artifact strength for the four benchmarks; each point is 50 replicate datasets. **b**, discrimination between artifact-free and artifact-bearing datasets: AUROC (filled) and AUPRC (open); the no-skill AUPRC is 0.8333. **c**, flag rate at strength zero, where silence is the correct answer, as registered with 50 replicates per point; the dashed line is the registered limit of 0.1, and rows follow panel **b**. The extended strength-zero runs are in Supplemental Note 3 and Supplemental Results SR6. Source data are provided.

**Figure S7. The literature survey, from records that passed title and abstract screening to analyses classified.** Of 942 records that passed screening, 367 had retrievable full text, a fixed-seed random sample of 120 was coded and 74 proved classifiable; their backgrounds are in main Fig. 1c. Screening and coding were performed by a language model and the survey is descriptive (Experimental Procedures; Supplemental Data 9 and 11). Source data are provided.
