# Supplemental Note 2. Training the audited ranker on public data

The ranker audited in Figure 7 and Supplemental Notes 9 and 10 is trained on one public persulfidation deposit and applied zero-shot to the other, in both directions: PRIDE PXD044043 (human) to PXD072089 (rice), and, pre-registered as a second direction before any rice-trained model existed, PXD072089 to PXD044043. Both cohorts carry T3-tier labels. Neither deposit's MaxQuant output contains a cysteine site table, so a cysteine is labeled when it is the only cysteine of a peptide identified in the enriched arm (Supplemental Notes 19 and 21); an unlabeled cysteine is not known to be unmodified.

**Species of PXD044043 (added in revision).** PRIDE lists the organism of PXD044043 as *Mus musculus*, but the deposit holds human data. Both deposited MaxQuant searches, of the enriched and of the input arm, used the human UniProt reference proteome (UP000005640) as the search database, and all twelve runs are named as THP-1 samples (control and LPS/IFN-γ-treated, three replicates each, in both arms); the source publication performed its proteome-wide analyses in THP-1 cells, a human monocytic cell line (Salti et al. 2024). All 1,519 cohort proteins are human UniProt entries, all present in the human reference proteome file used in our analyses, and none is an entry of the mouse reference proteome (UniProt UP000000589, release 2026_03). In a post hoc check, 974 of the 3,206 labeled cysteines (30%, in 650 proteins) lie in tryptic peptide segments of seven or more residues, read within the 15-residue window around the cysteine, that occur in no entry of the mouse reference proteome (mouse isoform sequences were not searched).

**Cohorts.** PXD044043: 18,567 cysteine residues in 1,519 proteins, 3,206 of them labeled; the human analyses of Supplemental Notes 9 to 12 use all of them (1,475 proteins carry both labeled and unlabeled cysteines). PXD072089: 5,598 residues in 640 proteins, 817 of them labeled (Supplemental Note 19 reproduces this label list from the deposit's peptide table). For the zero-shot ranker, 594 rice cysteines (80 labeled) in 59 proteins absent from the reference proteome file used were excluded, leaving 5,004 residues, 737 of them labeled, in 581 proteins; the zero-shot audits of Supplemental Notes 9 and 10 use these 5,004. The within-cohort refits of Supplemental Notes 11 and 12 were trained on all 5,598 rice residues (817 labeled, 640 proteins); their rice top-100 statistics and within-protein AUCs are computed on the 5,004 residues (578 proteins carry both classes), and the paired differences of Supplemental Note 11 are computed over the 637 proteins that carry both classes among all 5,598. Homology grouping was not used: a k-mer homology grouping exists only for the human cohort, where 66 of the 1,519 proteins fall in 29 multi-protein components. Cross-validation folds are protein-disjoint, verified disjoint at every split, but not verified homology-disjoint, so within-cohort intervals may be narrower than homology-component clustering would give. Because each direction is audited on the other species, no homology leakage between training and audit cohorts is possible.

**Procedure.** For each declared feature set (chemistry, 1046 columns; full, 1107 columns), a single LightGBM binary classifier -- not the three-member blend refit within each cohort in Supplemental Notes 11 and 12 -- is selected by 5-fold nested cross-validation on the training cohort's own folds: for each outer fold, a 3-configuration grid with early stopping (blocks of 25 rounds, patience 8 blocks) is searched on the remaining folds against one inner validation fold, and the selection is refit on the four non-test folds and scored on the held-out fold. The majority configuration and median round count across the five outer folds fix the final configuration, which is fit once on the complete training cohort and applied once, as raw scores, to every cysteine of the other cohort. Nothing is tuned on the audited cohort.

**Selected configurations and within-cohort check.** The out-of-fold AUC below is computed before any audited-cohort label is read and is reported only to show that the fitting produced a working model; it is not a study result.
