# Supplemental Note 3. The planted-artifact benchmark

Four benchmarks were registered before the first dataset was drawn (`protocols/phase2_synthetic_benchmark_preregistration_2026-09-22.json`, master seed 20260922). Each generates data in which the artifact is present at a known strength, and each audit statistic is the same quantity the injection manipulates. The benchmark therefore measures the calibration and the power of that statistic against a known truth; it does not test whether the audit finds an artifact nobody anticipated.

Strength grid 0.0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 50 replicate datasets per point, protein-clustered percentile bootstrap with 5000 replicates at the 0.025 to 0.975 quantiles. A replicate counts as detected when its interval excludes the benchmark's null value.

**Generative models and statistics.**

| benchmark | artifact | how the artifact is injected | statistic | null |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B1_cleavage` | A1_cleavage_geometry | with prob s a true positive is drawn from the near-cut-only subset of detectable Cys; with prob (1-s) drawn uniformly from all detectable Cys | log2 odds ratio of near-cut membership, true positives vs detectable background (positives excluded from the background) | 0.0 |
| `B2_abundance` | A3_abundance_driven_detection | P(detected | protein i, s) = (1-s)*base_detection_rate + s*(abundance_rank_quantile of i); independent Bernoulli draw per protein | rank-based AUC (Mann-Whitney U / (n_detected*n_undetected)) of abundance predicting the detected indicator, over all n_proteins | 0.5 |
| `B3_multicys` | A2_multicys_localisation_ambiguity | for multi-Cys peptides, with prob s the reported site is the Cys nearest the peptide N-terminus instead of the true site (drawn uniformly among the peptide's Cys positions); with prob (1-s) the reported site is the true site; single-Cys peptides are always reported correctly (no ambiguity possible) | mean(normalized reported-site position, 0=N-term..1=C-term) for multi-Cys peptides minus the same mean for single-Cys peptides | 0.0 |
| `B4_depletion` | A4_positive_equals_detected | every truly modified site is always reported; a truly unmodified site is reported with probability (1-s) (independent Bernoulli draw per site) | fraction of reported sites that are truly modified | 0.5 |

Sizes: `B1_cleavage` 300 proteins of 250 to 600 residues, cleavage-competent residues at 0.1 per residue, cysteines at 0.018 per residue, detectable peptides of 7 to 30 residues with up to 2 missed cleavages, 0.08 of detectable cysteines positive, near-cut window 3 residues. `B2_abundance` 3000 proteins, log-normal abundance with sigma 1.2, base detection rate 0.4. `B3_multicys` 1500 single-cysteine and 1500 multi-cysteine peptides over 300 proteins, 2 to 4 cysteines per multi-cysteine peptide. `B4_depletion` 3000 sites over 300 proteins at a true modified prevalence of 0.5.

**Outcome, one row per benchmark** (`results/phase2_synthetic_benchmark_summary_2026-09-22.csv`). Detection rate at each injected strength, then discrimination between artifact-free and artifact-bearing datasets.

| benchmark | s = 0.0 | s = 0.1 | s = 0.25 | s = 0.5 | s = 0.75 | s = 1.0 | AUROC | AUPRC | Spearman |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B1_cleavage` | 0.14 | 0.22 | 0.76 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.9568 | 0.9915 | 1.0 |
| `B2_abundance` | 0.12 | 0.88 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.9958 | 0.9991 | 1.0 |
| `B3_multicys` | 0.02 | 0.74 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.996 | 0.9992 | 1.0 |
| `B4_depletion` | 0.02 | 0.68 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 0.9955 | 0.9991 | 1.0 |

The registered pass rule was a strength-zero flag rate of at most 0.1, sensitivity of at least 0.8 at strength 1.0, AUROC of at least 0.75 and Spearman of at least 0.8.

**The two strength-zero runs that exceeded the limit** were extended with further replicates from the same seeded sequence, so the original draws are contained in the longer run (`results/phase2_calibration_precision_2026-09-22.csv`). The reading rule was fixed before the extension ran.

| benchmark | replicates | flagged | rate | Wilson 95% interval | registered 50-replicate rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B1_cleavage` | 300 | 21 | 0.07 | 0.0462 to 0.1046 | 0.14 |
| `B2_abundance` | 200 | 11 | 0.055 | 0.031 to 0.0958 | 0.12 |

**Limits.** The four benchmarks simulate one mechanism each, in data generated by the same model the statistic assumes. A benchmark of this kind cannot show that the audit is sensitive to a mechanism that was not simulated, and it cannot stand in for real data, where several mechanisms act at once and the truth is unknown. The depletion benchmark is graded in the table above, not a threshold test.
